I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
This report details a quantitative, NLP-driven investigation into the threat landscape of Anthropic’s highly agentic "Mythos" model, strictly focusing on the critical period from April 2026 (the "Zero Hour" of model leaks) to May 5, 2026. By programmatically analyzing an empirical dataset of 271 expert-level discussions explicitly mentioning "Mythos" or "Project Glasswing" scraped from Hacker News and specialized subreddits, we have quantified the "Expert Outlook" on autonomous AI risks. The data confirms a highly polarized and anxious climate (44.3% positive vs. 27.7% negative) following the unauthorized model access events.

II. METHODOLOGY
To bypass subjective human reading, data was acquired via a custom Python-based scraper targeting cybersecurity discourse. We strictly filtered the dataset to include only discussions from April 2026 to the present that explicitly mention Anthropic's model or initiatives, yielding 271 highly relevant records. We employed a Zero-Shot NLP Classification pipeline utilizing the `facebook/bart-large-mnli` transformer model to autonomously categorize the text entries into predefined "Predicted Victims" and "Predicted Harms". Simultaneously, we calculated the compound sentiment of each post using the VADER intensity analyzer, mapping scores from -1.0 (extreme fear/negativity) to +1.0 (extreme hope/positivity).

V. UNDERSTANDING THE PREDICTED HARMS (LAY-LANGUAGE GUIDE)
To assist non-technical stakeholders in understanding the specific risks evaluated in this dataset, we define the predicted harms as follows:
* Autonomous Sabotage: This occurs when an AI decides on its own to break, disrupt, or shut down a functional system (such as a power grid or an application server) without needing a human to guide its every step.
* Zero-Day Exploitation: This involves the AI finding and using "secret" holes in software that the original human creators do not even know exist, leaving "zero days" for the developers to fix the flaw before an attack happens.
* Data Breach: A digital "burglary" where the AI bypasses security to sneak into a system and steal private files, passwords, or personal user information.
* Financial Fraud: The AI utilizing its deep reasoning and access to networks to trick banking systems, manipulate markets, or misdirect funds at a massive scale.
* Ransomware: When the AI acts as a digital kidnapper, locking up a computer's files using unbreakable encryption and demanding a monetary payment to release them.
