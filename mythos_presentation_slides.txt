SLIDE-BY-SLIDE PRESENTATION DRAFT: IS MYTHOS DANGEROUS?

==================================================
SLIDE 1: PROJECT OVERVIEW - NLP-DRIVEN RISK ASSESSMENT
==================================================
* Goal: Quantify the real-world danger of Anthropic’s "Mythos" model using empirical data.
* Beyond Subjective Sentiment: Shifting from "How people feel" to "What experts predict."
* Core Focus: Programmatically identifying specific victims and technical harm vectors.
* Dataset: 369 expert-level discussions and post records scraped from Hacker News & Cybersecurity forums (April 2021 - May 2026).

==================================================
SLIDE 2: METHODOLOGY - THE "THREAT EXTRACTION" PIPELINE
==================================================
* Custom Scraper: Targeted extraction of expert discourse (titles + full comment bodies) via Algolia API.
* AI Engine: Zero-Shot NLP Classification utilizing the `facebook/bart-large-mnli` transformer model.
* Sentiment Analysis: VADER intensity mapping to score text from -1.0 (Extreme Fear) to +1.0 (Extreme Hope).
* Hardware Optimization: Pipeline executed on Apple M1 Pro GPU (MPS) for high-speed local inference.

==================================================
SLIDE 3: RESULTS (1) - OVERVIEW: THE WAR OF REALITIES
==================================================
* Polarization Discovered: The 369-post dataset is sharply split—49.6% Positive vs. 28.7% Negative.
* The Defensive View (Positive): High optimism surrounding the Mozilla Firefox audit (271 vulnerabilities autonomously fixed by Mythos).
* The Offensive Reality (Negative): Sharp anxiety following the April 2026 "Discord Sleuths" leak and reports of cybercriminal thefts.
* Conclusion: The technical community views Mythos as a neutral but highly potent "Force Multiplier" for both sides.

==================================================
SLIDE 4: RESULTS (2) - VICTIMOLOGY: VOLUME VS. VULNERABILITY
==================================================
* Individual Users: Represent the highest volume (310 mentions, 84.0% of data) but maintain a moderate positive sentiment (+0.20).
* Financial Institutions: The #1 Critical Outlier. Only 2.4% of mentions (9 total), but the ONLY category to register a Negative average sentiment score (-0.09).
* Systemic Targets: Critical Infrastructure (24 mentions, +0.11 score) and Government systems (14 mentions, +0.12 score) reflect heavy technical skepticism and high-stakes concern.
* The Defensive Buffer: Healthcare (+0.87 score) and SMEs (+0.30 score) are highly positive, viewed as potential beneficiaries of automated AI defense.

==================================================
* Is Mythos Dangerous? YES.
* The Smoking Gun: The "Deployment Time Lag" between AI capability and human organizational structure.
