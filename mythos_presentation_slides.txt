SLIDE-BY-SLIDE PRESENTATION DRAFT: IS MYTHOS DANGEROUS?
LING 575 Milestone 5 | Group 2

SLIDE 1: PROJECT OVERVIEW - NLP-DRIVEN RISK ASSESSMENT
* Goal: Quantify the real-world danger of Anthropic’s "Mythos" model.
* Beyond Sentiment: Shifting from "How people feel" to "What experts predict."
* Focus: Identifying specific victims and technical harm vectors.
* Dataset: 429 expert-level discussions from Hacker News & Cybersecurity forums.

SLIDE 2: METHODOLOGY - THE "THREAT EXTRACTION" PIPELINE
* Scraper: Targeted extraction of expert discourse (titles + full comment bodies).
* AI Engine: Zero-Shot NLP Classification (BART-Large-MNLI).
* Sentiment: VADER intensity mapping for outlook assessment.
* Hardware: Pipeline optimized on Apple M1 Pro GPU (MPS) for high-speed inference.

SLIDE 3: RESULTS (1) - OVERVIEW: THE WAR OF REALITIES
* Polarization: The dataset is split (50% Positive vs. 29% Negative).
* The Defensive View: High optimism surrounding the Mozilla Firefox audit (271 vulnerabilities fixed).
* The Offensive Reality: Sharp anxiety following the "Discord Sleuths" leak and $12M theft reports.
* Conclusion: Mythos is a "Force Multiplier" for both sides.

SLIDE 4: RESULTS (2) - VICTIMOLOGY: WHERE THE FEAR LIVES
* Individual Users: Represent the highest volume (368 mentions) but moderate sentiment.
* Financial Institutions: The #1 Critical Outlier. Only category with a Negative average sentiment score (-0.08).
* Systemic Targets: Critical Infrastructure and Government systems show technical skepticism and high stakes.
* Healthcare/SMEs: Viewed as potential beneficiaries of AI defense.

SLIDE 5: RESULTS (3) - HARM ANALYSIS: SABOTAGE OVER BREACHES
* Major Shift: 75% of extracted harms identify "Autonomous Sabotage" as the primary threat.
* The Escalation: "Zero-Day Exploitation" (Predicting invisible holes) is the #2 driver of technical anxiety.
* Expert Verdict: The community is less worried about stolen data and more worried about AI breaking functional systems.

SLIDE 6: RESULTS (4) - TEMPORAL TRENDS: THE 2026 CRISIS
* Baseline: Historic stability/optimism (2021-2024).
* The Explosion: Volatility began in late 2025 as agentic capabilities leaked.
* Zero Hour: April 2026 saw extreme sentiment crashes following model weight exposure.
* Current Outlook: Highly volatile. High optimism for patches (Mozilla) is being countered by fear of criminal misuse.

SLIDE 7: CONCLUSION - THE ASYMMETRY OF SPEED
* Is Mythos Dangerous? YES.
* The Smoking Gun: The "Deployment Time Lag."
* Discovery: Mythos identifies exploits in <15 seconds.
* Mitigation: Human patch cycles take 17+ days.
* Final Stance: The "danger" is the elimination of the human reaction window, creating permanent digital instability.
