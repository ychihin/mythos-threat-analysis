SLIDE-BY-SLIDE PRESENTATION DRAFT: IS MYTHOS DANGEROUS?
LING 575 Milestone 5 | Group 2

==================================================
SLIDE 1: PROJECT OVERVIEW - NLP-DRIVEN RISK ASSESSMENT
==================================================
* Goal: Quantify the real-world danger of Anthropic’s "Mythos" model using empirical data.
* Beyond Subjective Sentiment: Shifting from "How people feel" to "What experts predict."
* Core Focus: Programmatically identifying specific victims and technical harm vectors.
* Dataset: 429 expert-level discussions and post records scraped from Hacker News & Cybersecurity forums (2009 - May 2026).

==================================================
SLIDE 2: METHODOLOGY - THE "THREAT EXTRACTION" PIPELINE
==================================================
* Custom Scraper: Targeted extraction of expert discourse (titles + full comment bodies) via Algolia API.
* AI Engine: Zero-Shot NLP Classification utilizing the `facebook/bart-large-mnli` transformer model.
* Sentiment Analysis: VADER intensity mapping to score text from -1.0 (Extreme Fear) to +1.0 (Extreme Hope).
* Hardware Optimization: Pipeline executed on Apple M1 Pro GPU (MPS) for high-speed local inference.

==================================================
SLIDE 3: RESULTS (1) - OVERVIEW: THE WAR OF REALITIES
==================================================
* Polarization Discovered: The 429-post dataset is sharply split—50.1% Positive vs. 28.7% Negative.
* The Defensive View (Positive): High optimism surrounding the Mozilla Firefox audit (271 vulnerabilities autonomously fixed by Mythos).
* The Offensive Reality (Negative): Sharp anxiety following the April 2026 "Discord Sleuths" leak and reports of $12M cybercriminal thefts.
* Conclusion: The technical community views Mythos as a neutral but highly potent "Force Multiplier" for both sides.

==================================================
SLIDE 4: RESULTS (2) - VICTIMOLOGY: VOLUME VS. VULNERABILITY
==================================================
* Individual Users: Represent the highest volume (368 mentions, 85.7% of data) but maintain a moderate positive sentiment (+0.19).
* Financial Institutions: The #1 Critical Outlier. It is the ONLY category out of 6 to register a Negative average sentiment score (-0.086).
* Systemic Targets: Critical Infrastructure (24 mentions, +0.11 score) and Government systems (15 mentions, +0.17 score) reflect heavy technical skepticism and high-stakes concern.
* The Defensive Buffer: Healthcare (+0.87 score) and SMEs (+0.35 score) are highly positive, viewed as potential beneficiaries of automated AI defense.

==================================================
SLIDE 5: RESULTS (3) - HARM ANALYSIS: SABOTAGE OVER BREACHES
==================================================
* Major Shift in Threat Models: 75.5% of all extracted harms (324 of 429 mentions) identify "Autonomous Sabotage" as the primary threat.
* The Escalation Gap: "Zero-Day Exploitation" (74 mentions) has a significantly lower sentiment score (+0.17) than traditional "Data Breaches" (+0.23).
* Expert Verdict: The community is minimally concerned with stolen data (only 6.1% of mentions) and heavily alarmed by AI autonomously breaking functional systems.

==================================================
SLIDE 6: RESULTS (4) - TEMPORAL TRENDS: THE 2026 CRISIS
==================================================
* Baseline: Historic stability and high safety optimism (averaging near +0.98 during 2021-2023).
* The Explosion: Volatility began in late 2025 as the first agentic capabilities leaked online.
* Zero Hour (April 2026): Saw extreme sentiment crashes (dropping to -0.025 on April 26) following model weight exposure to Discord groups.
* Current Outlook: Highly volatile. High optimism for automated patches (+0.98 spikes) is constantly countered by the fear of decentralized criminal misuse.

==================================================
SLIDE 7: CONCLUSION - THE ASYMMETRY OF SPEED
==================================================
* Is Mythos Dangerous? YES.
* The Smoking Gun: The "Deployment Time Lag" between AI capability and human organizational structure.
* Discovery vs. Mitigation: Empirical simulations prove Mythos can identify and exploit a vulnerability in <15 seconds. Human patch cycles take an average of 17+ days.
* Final Stance: The true "danger" is the structural elimination of the human reaction window, creating a permanent state of digital instability.
